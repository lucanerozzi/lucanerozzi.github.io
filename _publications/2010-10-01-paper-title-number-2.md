This study examines the impact of recent CAP reforms on farmland allocation and related implications on farm profitability, in a sample of highly specialized cereal farms located in intensive agricultural areas of Northern Italy. Using Integer Linear Programming models, we simulate farmers decision-making under different CAP frameworks, considering 2014-2020, 2021-2023, and 2023-2027 environmental constraints changes.
