As a Teaching Assistant for the Mathematical Analysis II (Calculus II) course (Classe di laurea L-8), I worked alongside the main professor to guide undergraduate engineering students through advanced mathematical concepts.

**Key Responsibilities:**
* Led weekly exercise sessions focusing on multi-variable calculus, differential equations, and integration.
* Mentored students to improve their analytical problem-solving skills ahead of the final exams.
* Participated in the administration and evaluation of the exams.
